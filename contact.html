Frequently Asked Questions

Can I contribute notes or question papers?

Yes! If you have well-structured handwritten or typed lecture notes, syllabus guides, or previous year university question papers (PYQs), please email them to asnstudios.app@gmail.com with subject and unit details.

Are all resources on BTechBook free?

Yes. All lecture notes, formula sheets, and study materials published on BTechBook.in are 100% free for educational, non-commercial use by students and faculty.

How do I report an error in a document?

If you discover an error, broken link, or outdated syllabus in any of our notes, please send us an email specifying the subject name, unit number, and the correction needed.
