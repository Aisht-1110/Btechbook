Disclaimer & DMCA Policy

Last Updated: September 2025

Educational Purpose Only: BTechBook.in is a non-profit educational platform created to support engineering students in their exam preparation. All study notes, syllabus references, and question papers are provided purely for academic reference.

1. Non-Commercial Academic Fair Use

All materials, handwritten lecture summaries, formula sheets, and study aids available on BTechBook.in are shared under the principles of educational Fair Use. We do not sell or monetize access to any study material. Access to all resources is 100% free of charge for all students, educators, and researchers.

2. Third-Party Content & External Links

Some study materials may reference standard university curricula, textbook chapters, or open-source academic documents. BTechBook.in does not host pirated copyrighted materials on our own servers. Files are indexed from community submissions and publicly accessible academic repositories.

3. DMCA Takedown & Copyright Notice

BTechBook respects the intellectual property rights of all authors, publishers, and educators. If you are the copyright owner of any material found on this website and wish to have it removed or attributed differently, please submit a notice with the following details:

  • The exact URL(s) of the material in question.
  • Proof of ownership or authorized representation of the copyright owner.
  • Your full name, organization, and contact email address.

Send all copyright-related requests directly to: asnstudios.app@gmail.com.

Upon receipt of a valid notice, we will review and remove the requested content within 24 to 48 business hours.

4. Accuracy of Educational Content

While we strive to ensure that all lecture notes and formulas are accurate and aligned with university syllabi (e.g., AICTE, Anna University, VTU, AKTU, JNTU), BTechBook makes no warranties regarding the absolute completeness or error-free nature of student-compiled notes. Students are advised to cross-verify with their official university textbooks and professors.
